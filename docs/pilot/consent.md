# The review pilot: consent

*Read this before you start. The pilot page shows the same text on its first screen.*

## What this is for
Earshot turns a salesperson's spoken debrief into proposed CRM changes, and a person reviews each one before anything is saved. This pilot measures two things about that review:
- how long a card takes to decide;
- whether reviewers catch changes that were matched to the wrong company or person.

It tests the software, not you. There are no right speeds and nothing to prepare.

## What you'll do
- Review 3 practice cards, then about 40 proposed CRM changes from 10 short sales debriefs, on a laptop in Chrome.
- Accept, edit, reject, or set aside each card, by keyboard or mouse.
- It takes about 30 minutes.

The debriefs are synthetic: they were written for testing, and the companies and people in them are made up. You don't speak, no voice is processed, and the cards have no audio.

## What's logged
For each card:
- your decision, and the record you chose, if you chose one;
- whether a key, a click, or a touch made the decision;
- how long the card was active on screen before you decided;
- the card as it was shown to you.

The file also says when the session started and finished, which browser engine you used, and whether it was a phone or tablet. That's all. There's no name, email, account, or IP address, and no audio or screen recording.

## Where it goes
- The page keeps your progress in that browser tab only, and sends nothing to any server.
- At the end, you download a results file and give it to the person running your session. It's plain text (JSON), and you're welcome to read it first.
- Results are reported only in aggregate, as medians and counts, with participants labelled P1 to P5.

## Stopping and deleting
- You can stop at any time without giving a reason. Close the tab and nothing is kept.
- If you've already handed over your file, ask the person running your session to delete it. They will, and will tell you when it's done.
- Files are deleted once the project's Phase 8 report is written, and within 12 months at the latest.

## Questions
Ask the person running your session, before, during, or after it. Their contact details are on your invitation.

## Agreeing
If you agree to take part on these terms, choose "I agree" on the pilot page's first screen.
