# Working in this repo

Earshot v2 is a pnpm + Turborepo monorepo with a Python lab. Read `ROADMAP.md` and `docs/adr/` before changing architecture.

## Map

- `apps/web`: Next.js 16 on Cloudflare Workers via OpenNext; also the review inbox at `/inbox` (`components/inbox/`, `lib/inbox/`: the loader, cards in lists with roving focus, focus-scoped single-key shortcuts that Settings → Review turns off, the review clock, reviews through the web route, and "accept all" with its inline checkpoint, which takes only what `tierOf` leaves unmarked, as one batch with one undo; ADR-0035), tested by the Playwright `inbox` project. Diff cards (Phase 5 WP12; ADR-0035, ADR-0008): the loader reads each update's value when the rep spoke (`base`, by `valueAt`'s rule over the mirror's history) beside its value now (`lib/inbox/crm.ts`), and re-links new records still open at load when a matching record arrived after the rep spoke (`lib/inbox/relink.ts`: entity linker v1 over the mirror, with the session's focus account and earlier links, `@earshot/agent/link-context`); the 3-way card ("Discovery when you spoke · Proposal in the CRM now · you said Negotiation": Keep mine, Keep the CRM's, Edit) and the re-link offer are loud at review time though neither is a flag, so "accept all" leaves them; and the demo controls in a card's More menu, only on demo records ("Simulate a CRM change", "Add a matching record": `lib/inbox/demo.ts`, `app/actions/inbox-demo.ts`, which the inbox page hands to its view). The review engine (`useReviewList`) also drives the capture screen's tray, on the inbox's card, over the session socket until the seal and the web route after it; the header's "Inbox" and the home page show the count of what waits for review (`lib/inbox/count.ts`). Evidence clips (`lib/evidence/`, `components/evidence/`; ADR-0036): the capture controller keeps each turn's audio on the device while Settings → Capture's switch is on, the inbox's and the tray's cards (`EvidenceCard`) get play buttons and one line for each reason there's no audio, and sign-out deletes every clip; the pilot's cards never offer playback. The recap (`components/recap/`, `lib/recap/`; ADR-0037): atop its debrief's group in the inbox and on the capture screen once the debrief ends, each sentence a Collapsible that opens the words it quotes (with their play buttons), the lens collapsed ("MEDDIC: 2 of 6 mentioned"), and Settings → Capture's "Recap lens", per device, which the capture controller sends with `end` beside the session's LLM key. Approval policies (`components/policies/`, `lib/policies/`; ADR-0034): Settings → Review's per-type "Auto-approve" switches and the fields that always need review, saved through `PUT /api/policies` (`app/api/policies/`) behind a step-up, a sign-in from the last 5 minutes (`STEP_UP_SECONDS` in `lib/auth/fresh.ts`; "Confirm with your passkey", or an email link), each change a new version with a `policy.changed` audit entry and checkpoint; `POST /api/sessions` signs the workspace's latest version into each new session, which the capture controller sends in `start.policy`; and Reviewed shows each auto-approval as "Approved by your policy: notes" with Undo. Corrections and follow-up questions (Phase 5 WP13; ADR-0038): c's panel gains a field picker (the record preselected on a card about one) and a text box whose Enter sends the words, through `lib/inbox/corrections.ts`'s sinks: the inbox's to `POST /api/sessions/:id/corrections` (`app/api/sessions/[id]/corrections/`, `lib/reviews/corrections.ts`: sealed sessions only, `correctProposal` over the workspace's mirror in the web Worker, the session's limits again, and a per-viewer request limit in production), the tray's on `correct` over the socket, with a microphone in push-to-talk (the capture controller's `spokenCorrections` arms the next turn as the correction); the corrected card takes the original's place and focus, and follow-up cards (`components/inbox/followup-card.tsx`) sit after their proposal as focus stops, c to answer and r to skip. The pilot passes no correction sink, so its cards are unchanged. And the review pilot's static page at `/pilot` (`components/pilot/`, `lib/pilot/`: consent from docs/pilot/consent.md, then the practice and pilot.v1's 40 cards on the same engine with a local sink that folds reviews in the page, progress in `sessionStorage`, and a results file with nothing else; ADR-0039), tested by the Playwright `pilot` project, which also checks it requests only static files, and by its scripted reviewer, the `reviewer` project (WP8, gates G1a and G2a: `/pilot?p=ci` by keyboard on a fake clock with the manifest's right answers; every card's markers in the accessibility tree and on screen, keys per decision, focus never on `<body>`, axe in every state it reaches, and the KLM estimate per card against `packages/evals/baselines/pilot-klm.json`, which `EARSHOT_PILOT_KLM=record` re-records on purpose; its report goes to CI's job summary)
- `apps/edge`: Hono Worker that owns the Durable Objects: the session socket, `SessionDO` (the SQLite-first event log, resume, turns, the second witness's comparison, the cost meter, the tray's reviews and snoozes, the recap right after `session.ended`, in the serial queue within 15 s on the key `end.recap` carries, and the seal after it, its last event; and approval policies: `start.policy` verified against the session's id with the ticket secret, then every new proposal a rule matches approved in its turn's own transaction as a `mode: "policy"` review, never without an audit key; ADR-0034; and corrections and follow-up questions: `followup.asked` after each turn's proposals, `correct` read by `correctProposal` over `start`'s records into a proposal that supersedes the one corrected, with typed words only on `correct`, limits per window, per session, in characters, and in a chain's depth, and a cached fold, and `followup` skipping a question; ADR-0038), the Queue consumer that flushes sessions to Postgres with audit rows for their reviews, and the `purgeSessions` and `sealSessions` RPCs (docs/adr/0024, 0029, 0032, 0037)
- `packages/agent`: the turn pipeline for `SessionDO`: the proposal extractor (tool schema v2 and a prompt with no CRM list: the model quotes the words that named each record), Anthropic and Groq adapters on AI SDK 7 (with an opt-in prompt cache, which the live smoke eval uses), `understand()` (evidence by quotation, entity linker v1, the normalizers, and the faithfulness check; ADR-0027), the second witness's comparison for a turn (`witnessTurn`, with the link context `runTurn` hands back; ADR-0029), the session's recap (`@earshot/agent/recap`: one forced `submit_recap` call on the same adapters, `runRecap` with its 15 s deadline, the checks in code that keep only sentences whose quotes are in the transcript, and the lens files, `./recap/lenses`, which the web app imports alone; ADR-0037), fetch-level cassettes (`@earshot/agent/testing`, with a scripted recap model for the edge's test and e2e Workers), and the linker's context (`@earshot/agent/link-context`: the kinds each proposal type can target, the mention as the linker reads it, and earlier links), which the web app imports alone to re-link at review time
- `packages/core`: Zod contracts, their JSON Schema exports (`schemas/`), and shared fixtures, `ApprovalPolicyRules` among them (with `checkPolicyRules`, the writer's rules the schema can't express); also the session socket protocol (whose key guard allows a key-shaped string only at `turn_end.key` and `end.recap.key`), session tickets, the approval policy's snapshot (`signPolicySnapshot`, `verifyPolicySnapshot`: HMAC with the ticket secret over `canonicalJson` of the policy and the session id; ADR-0034), the review, policy, and correction routes' request contracts, `correctionProposalId` (a correction's superseding proposal's id, from SHA-256; ADR-0038), and `uuidv7()`
- `packages/db`: Drizzle schema, migrations, the client, and `withTenant` (row-level security; pgTAP tests in `test/pgtap`); also the audit chain, which a trigger hashes per workspace, with `recordAudit`, signed checkpoints (`writeCheckpoint`), `exportAuditChain`, and the offline check (`verifyAuditExport`, `pnpm --filter @earshot/db db:audit-verify <export.json>`; ADR-0033); review writes after the seal (`writeSessionReviews`, `recordReviewAudit`, the repair path's `sealSessionLog`, and `importSessionLog` for sample sessions; ADR-0032); and approval policies (`approval_policies`, migration 0016, append-only for the app; `readApprovalPolicy`, and `writeApprovalPolicy`, which appends a version with its `policy.changed` audit entry and checkpoint; ADR-0034); and the mirror's history (`crm_entity_changes`, migration 0017, append-only for the app, `source` `demo` or `sync`; `valueAt`, `readFieldHistory`, and `recordCrmChanges` in `src/crm-history.ts`) with the demo CRM's controls that write it (`moveDemoField`, `addDemoRecord`, demo records only; ADR-0008); and corrections after the seal (`writeSessionCorrection`, `writeFollowupSkip` in `src/corrections.ts`, under the session's advisory lock; migration 0018's `utterances.input` and `proposals.followups`; ADR-0038)
- `packages/events`: the session event store: payload registry and upcasters, projections (the session's folds its one `recap.created` between the end and the seal; ADR-0037), deterministic replay, JSONL, the rules every writer of reviews and snoozes keeps (`checkReview`, `checkReviews`, `checkSnooze`; ADR-0032; a batch or a policy never approves what was seen loud at review time, `seenLoudAtReview`, and a re-link widens only a new record's choices, Phase 5 WP12), `policyRuleFor`, which rule of an approval policy approves a proposal as it's created (ADR-0034), and the correction rules (`checkCorrection`, `correctionBudget`, `checkFollowupSkip`, and `CORRECTION_LIMITS`), with each proposal's follow-up questions on its view, moving to the proposal that supersedes it (ADR-0038)
- `packages/keys`: AI key handling: on-device storage, the envelope-encrypted vault, validation, and log redaction; also the audit checkpoint signer (Ed25519, `EARSHOT_AUDIT_SIGNING_KEY`; `pnpm --filter @earshot/keys audit-key:new` makes a key; ADR-0033)
- `packages/voice`: the browser voice runtime: capture and resampling, the speech-to-text adapters, the Debrief engine (and each turn's audio for the second witness), the second witness itself (Groq Whisper on the user's key, from the browser; ADR-0029), the client session runtime (`./session`: `SessionClient`, whose `end` takes the recap's request, its key held apart in the in-memory outbox like a turn's, and whose `sendCorrection` and `sendFollowupSkip` go over the socket and, for typed corrections and skips after the seal, to the corrections route; the engine bridge, which sends an armed correction's turn as `correct`; the store the screen subscribes to; and `SessionWitness`, which sends each turn's witness), the price table and pure machines (`./prices`, `./machines`, no DOM), evidence clips on the device (`./evidence`: each turn's audio in IndexedDB `earshot-evidence`, AES-GCM under its own non-extractable key with each record bound to its id, never played after 24 hours and purged lazily, and the Web Audio player that plays a field's span padded by 250 ms; ADR-0036), and fakes, including an in-memory SessionDO and a scripted witness (`./testing`); `pnpm test` runs in Node, `pnpm test:browser` in Chromium
- `packages/demo-crm`: the deterministic demo CRM, with confusable-name groups
- `packages/understanding`: the understanding step, pure TypeScript with no DOM or Node APIs (the edge Worker imports it): the name toolkit and homophone list (`./names`), entity linker v1 (`./link`: the per-session index, candidates, features, a calibrated logistic model, the link / ask / new decision, and its frozen `params.v1.json`; ADR-0028), the second witness's comparison (`./witness`: the unbiased reading by word timings or alignment, conflicts at code distance θ_d, downgrades and flags), the value normalizers (`./normalize`: dates with chrono-node's English parser, the call's offset from `Intl`, and Earshot's weekday and end-of-period rules; amounts with currencies; NegEx-style negation; self-corrections), the faithfulness check (`./faithfulness`: each field against its quote by field type, replacing a date, time, or amount only when its reading is unique; ADR-0030), the likely-edit tiers (`./tiers`: `tierOf`, loud, quiet, or none from a proposal's flags and decisions, which every writer, the cards, and the evals share; ADR-0035), corrections (`./correct`: `correctProposal`, a correction's words read into a superseding proposal by the linker and the normalizers, with no model call and no key), and follow-up questions (`./followups`: the base rules, at most two a turn, none on a loud card; ADR-0038)
- `packages/evals`: eval harness v1 (ADR-0031): 200 debrief scripts with ground truth, the 30 hand-written ones the fake-microphone eval renders (`debriefs/`, format v1, upgraded in code) and 170 from a seeded grammar (`debriefs/generated/`, format v2, with what each witness heard; `pnpm --filter @earshot/evals debriefs:gen`, `src/grammar/`), their schema and coverage rules, the understanding eval over all 200 (`@earshot/evals/understanding`: offline cassettes with seeded, labelled model-like errors, the report, the likely-edit tiers' shares, surfacing, and precision, and CI's `checks` gate, `eval:understanding --check` against `baselines/understanding.json` and the tiers' budgets: loud at most an interim 18%, any card marker at most 35%, surfacing at least 95%), the session context their cassettes are keyed on, the cassettes for both LLM adapters (`@earshot/evals/cassettes`; regenerate with `pnpm --filter @earshot/evals cassettes:gen`), and the fake-microphone eval's turn checks, report, baselines (`baseline:update`), and live budget guard. The eval itself is `apps/web/e2e/eval/` (the `capture-smoke` and `capture-eval` Playwright projects). Also the confusable-name set for entity linking (`linking/confusable.v1.jsonl`, frozen; ADR-0028), its seeded mishearing simulator (`@earshot/evals/channel`), its harness (`@earshot/evals/linking`; `pnpm --filter @earshot/evals eval:linking --split test`, `--linker v1` for linker v1 with the second witness and its ablations; CI's `checks` job runs `--linker v1 --split test --check`, gate G1a, against `baselines/linking.json`), and linker v1's fit on the calibration split (`pnpm --filter @earshot/evals linker:fit`, which writes `params.v1.json`, θ_d included). Also the live smoke eval, off until the user turns it on (`@earshot/evals/smoke`; ADR-0031): `smoke.json`'s 30 scripts (re-picked by `smoke:select`) as text through a live model, paced, budget-guarded, and gated against `baselines/smoke-<provider>.json`. `eval:smoke --estimate` and `--offline` cost $0; `eval:smoke` and `baseline:smoke` call the model and need the user's approval. CI's `.github/workflows/eval-smoke.yml` runs it. Each script's run also writes its recap, gated on recap evidence and the drop rate against the baseline (Phase 5 WP10). Also the recap eval (`@earshot/evals/recap`; ADR-0037): recap cassettes for all 200 debriefs built offline from the ground truth, with seeded, labelled bad sentences and invented lens quotes (`cassettes/recap/`; `pnpm --filter @earshot/evals recap:gen`, which a test holds to the committed files), and CI's `checks` gate, `eval:recap --check` against `baselines/recap.json`: every kept sentence with evidence, every seeded bad sentence dropped, every recap with a headline; and the recap's live check (`recap:live`, one recap per provider under $0.01, off unless `EARSHOT_RECAP_LIVE=1` and the `EVAL_*_KEY`s are set; it calls the models and needs the user's approval). Also the review pilot's kit (`@earshot/evals/pilot`; ADR-0039): `pilot.v1` (`pilot/pilot.v1/`: 10 sealed session logs with 40 proposals, 4 flagged and 4 silent seeded wrong accounts, a 3-card practice, the participants' orders, and a manifest whose `sha256` the pre-registration names; `pnpm --filter @earshot/evals pilot:gen`, which a test holds to the committed files byte for byte, and which must never run again once the pilot has begun), the pre-registered analysis (`pnpm --filter @earshot/evals pilot:analyze results/*.json`, markdown), and the scripted reviewer's pure parts (`klm.ts`, `reviewer.ts`): its oracle from the manifest, the key budgets, the KLM estimate per card (words are what the card shows when its deciding key is pressed, without buttons or key labels), the gate against `baselines/pilot-klm.json` (no card more than 10% slower, and the median at most 4.0 s or, while the set misses that, its baseline's), the flag-follower bound, and the report.
- `packages/tokens`, `packages/ui`: design tokens and components (on Base UI, `Collapsible` among them)
- `packages/tsconfig`: shared TypeScript presets
- `lab/`: Python 3.13 (uv): generated Pydantic contracts, tests, experiments, and the Kokoro renderer for the eval WAVs (`scripts/render_debriefs.py`, in the `tts` group; `--stub` renders tones for local plumbing checks, never for evaluation)

## Commands

- `pnpm install`, then `pnpm dev`
- `pnpm check` (Biome), `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm build`
- `pnpm contracts:gen`: regenerate `packages/core/schemas` and the lab's Pydantic models

## Gates (run before every commit)

- `pnpm check && pnpm typecheck && pnpm test`
- Changed a contract in `packages/core`? Run `pnpm contracts:gen` and commit the generated files.
- Architectural change? Add an ADR in `docs/adr/`.
- Never commit secrets. `.env*` and `.dev.vars*` stay local.

## Conventions

- PR titles are Conventional Commits; PRs are squash-merged.
- TypeScript is strict ESM. No enums, namespaces, or parameter properties (`erasableSyntaxOnly`).
- Internal packages export TypeScript source (`"exports": { ".": "./src/index.ts" }`) and have no build step.
- Node 26 (`.nvmrc`), pnpm 12 (`packageManager`), uv for Python.
